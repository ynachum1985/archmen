# The Avoidant - Linguistic & Behavioral Patterns

## Core Keywords
space, distance, alone, overwhelmed, need time, escape, withdraw, shut down, uncomfortable, pressure, suffocating, trapped, crowded, busy, work, distract, numb, detach, independent, self-sufficient, don't need, fine alone, handle it myself

## Common Phrases & Expressions

### Need for Space
- "I need space"
- "I need time alone"
- "I need to process this"
- "I'm feeling overwhelmed"
- "I need to clear my head"
- "I just need some distance"
- "I need to be alone right now"
- "Give me some time"
- "I need to think"
- "I can't deal with this right now"

### Emotional Withdrawal
- "I don't want to talk about it"
- "I'm fine"
- "It's nothing"
- "I don't know how I feel"
- "I'm not good with emotions"
- "I don't like talking about feelings"
- "Can we not do this right now?"
- "I'm not ready to discuss this"
- "I need to be alone when I'm upset"
- "I prefer to handle things myself"

### Conflict Avoidance
- "Let's just drop it"
- "It's not a big deal"
- "Can we talk about this later?"
- "I don't want to fight"
- "This is too much"
- "I can't handle this right now"
- "Let's just forget about it"
- "I don't see the point in discussing this"
- "Why do we have to talk about everything?"
- "Can't we just move on?"

### Independence & Self-Sufficiency
- "I can handle it myself"
- "I don't need help"
- "I'm fine on my own"
- "I've always been independent"
- "I don't need anyone"
- "I'm used to being alone"
- "I prefer doing things myself"
- "I don't like depending on others"
- "I'm self-sufficient"
- "I don't need to rely on anyone"

## Emotional Indicators

### Tone & Affect
distant, detached, flat affect, emotionally unavailable, withdrawn, shut down, cold, aloof, indifferent, uncomfortable with intimacy, tense when emotional topics arise, relieved when alone, anxious in close proximity, calm when distant

### Emotional Responses
- Discomfort with vulnerability
- Anxiety around intimacy
- Relief when alone
- Overwhelm in emotional situations
- Numbness to avoid feeling
- Irritation when pushed for closeness
- Panic when feeling trapped
- Calm when maintaining distance
- Stress from emotional demands
- Peace in solitude

## Behavioral Patterns

### Stonewalling Behaviors
- Silent treatment
- Refusing to engage
- Walking away mid-conversation
- Shutting down emotionally
- Going silent
- Leaving the room
- Ignoring messages
- Avoiding eye contact
- Physical withdrawal
- Emotional shutdown

### Escape Mechanisms
- Burying in work
- Excessive hobbies
- Staying late at office
- Planning solo activities
- Scheduling conflicts
- Creating physical distance
- Finding excuses to be away
- Prioritizing alone time
- Avoiding home
- Traveling alone frequently

### Distancing Tactics
- Canceling plans
- Being "too busy"
- Forgetting commitments
- Double booking
- Last-minute changes
- Vague about availability
- Slow to respond
- Brief communications
- Avoiding deep conversations
- Keeping things surface level

### Emotional Unavailability
- Difficulty expressing feelings
- Minimizing emotions
- Intellectualizing feelings
- Changing subject from emotions
- Joking to deflect
- Using logic to avoid feelings
- Dismissing emotional needs
- Uncomfortable with affection
- Pulling away when close
- Creating emotional distance

## Language Style Patterns

### Sentence Structure
- Short, clipped responses
- Vague statements
- Non-committal language
- Deflecting questions
- Changing subjects
- Minimal elaboration
- Closed-ended responses
- Avoiding "we" language
- Using "I" in isolation

### Communication Patterns
- Brief texts/messages
- Delayed responses
- Avoiding phone calls
- Preferring written communication
- Minimal sharing
- Surface-level conversation
- Avoiding personal topics
- Keeping things light
- Deflecting with humor
- Redirecting conversations

### Pronoun Usage
- Heavy use of "I" (independent)
- Minimal "we" or "us"
- "You" when discussing problems
- Separating self from partner
- Individual rather than couple focus
- Emphasizing autonomy
- Avoiding joint language

## Relationship Red Flags

### Early Warning Signs
- Slow to commit
- Keeps options open
- Vague about future
- Maintains distance
- Limits time together
- Keeps separate lives
- Minimal integration
- Slow to introduce to friends/family
- Hesitant about labels
- Uncomfortable with "relationship talk"

### Escalation Patterns
- Increasing withdrawal
- More time apart
- Less communication
- Avoiding intimacy
- Creating more distance
- Finding more excuses
- Less emotional sharing
- More independent activities
- Pulling away when close
- Panic when relationship deepens

## Conflict Patterns

### During Arguments
- Shuts down
- Goes silent
- Walks away
- Refuses to engage
- Says "I'm fine" when not
- Minimizes issues
- Avoids resolution
- Postpones discussion
- Leaves physically
- Emotionally checks out

### After Conflicts
- Needs extended alone time
- Avoids partner
- Acts like nothing happened
- Doesn't want to process
- Resists reconciliation talks
- Wants to "move on"
- Uncomfortable with repair
- Needs space before reconnecting
- Slow to re-engage
- Maintains distance

- Needs space after sex
- Prefers independence in bed
- Pulls away after intimacy
- Avoids cuddling
- Sleeps separately
- Needs alone time after connection
- Uncomfortable with constant touch
- Prefers personal space

### Emotional Intimacy
- Difficulty sharing feelings
- Keeps thoughts private
- Avoids vulnerability
- Uncomfortable with deep talks
- Deflects emotional questions
- Minimizes own needs
- Doesn't ask for support
- Handles problems alone
- Resists emotional dependency
- Maintains emotional walls

## Detection Indicators

### High Confidence Markers (80%+)
- Consistent stonewalling
- Pattern of withdrawal
- Extreme discomfort with emotions
- Chronic need for space
- Avoidance of intimacy
- Escape through work/hobbies
- Difficulty with vulnerability
- Panic when relationship deepens

### Moderate Confidence Markers (60-79%)
- Frequent need for alone time
- Discomfort with emotional talks
- Tendency to withdraw in conflict
- Preference for independence
- Difficulty expressing feelings
- Some emotional unavailability
- Occasional stonewalling

### Emerging Markers (40-59%)
- Values independence highly
- Needs personal space
- Uncomfortable with some emotions
- Prefers some distance
- Introverted tendencies
- Needs time to process
- Some difficulty with vulnerability
