# The Narcissist - Linguistic & Behavioral Patterns

## Core Keywords
control, authority, right, superior, deserve, dominate, power, command, rule, best, always, never, should, must, entitled, special, important, better, perfect, flawless, exceptional, admiration, respect, recognition, validation, attention, center, focus, me, I, my, mine

## Common Phrases & Expressions

### Control & Authority
- "I always need to be right"
- "I know what's best"
- "I should be in charge"
- "I need to control the decisions"
- "I'll decide what's best for us"
- "My way is the right way"
- "I deserve to lead"
- "I'm the one who knows"
- "Trust me, I know better"
- "I have the final say"

### Dismissiveness & Invalidation
- "She's too sensitive"
- "She's overreacting"
- "You're being emotional"
- "That's not a real problem"
- "You're making a big deal out of nothing"
- "I'm just being logical"
- "You're too dramatic"
- "You're imagining things"
- "That's not what happened"
- "You're remembering it wrong"

### Entitlement & Superiority
- "I deserve better"
- "I'm special"
- "I'm more important"
- "People should respect me"
- "I've earned this"
- "I'm above that"
- "That's beneath me"
- "I shouldn't have to deal with this"
- "I'm too good for this"
- "I deserve admiration"

### Blame Shifting
- "It's your fault"
- "You made me do this"
- "If you hadn't..."
- "You're the problem"
- "I wouldn't have to if you..."
- "You're the one who..."
- "This is because of you"
- "You caused this"

## Emotional Indicators

### Tone & Affect
dismissive, defensive, entitled, arrogant, condescending, superior, contemptuous, irritated when challenged, impatient with others, annoyed by criticism, cold when not getting attention, charming when seeking admiration, grandiose, self-important, haughty, disdainful

### Emotional Responses
- Rage when criticized
- Indifference to others' pain
- Excessive pride
- Envy of others
- Belief others are envious
- Lack of empathy
- Emotional manipulation
- Withholding affection as punishment
- Love bombing when needed
- Sudden coldness

## Behavioral Patterns

- "You're remembering it wrong"
- "That never happened"
- "You're crazy"
- "You're too sensitive"
- "I never said that"
- "You're making things up"
- Rewriting history
- Denying obvious facts
- Making partner doubt reality
- Twisting words

- Making all major decisions
- Financial control
- Isolating from friends/family
- Monitoring activities
- Demanding constant updates
- Controlling appearance
- Dictating schedule
- Limiting independence
- Punishing autonomy
- Creating dependency

### Manipulation Strategies
- Silent treatment
- Withholding affection
- Guilt tripping
- Playing victim
- Triangulation
- Comparing to others
- Conditional love
- Moving goalposts
- Double standards
- Projection

### Relationship Dynamics
- Needs constant admiration
- Lacks empathy
- Exploits others
- Envious or believes others envious
- Arrogant behaviors
- Sense of entitlement
- Requires excessive admiration
- Takes advantage of others
- Unwilling to recognize others' needs
- Shows arrogant behaviors

## Language Style Patterns

### Sentence Structure
- Absolute statements: "always", "never", "everyone", "no one"
- Authoritative declarations
- Imperatives and commands
- Rhetorical questions to dismiss
- Interrupting others
- Talking over people
- Dominating conversations
- Redirecting to self

### Pronoun Usage
- Excessive use of "I", "me", "my", "mine"
- Minimal use of "we", "us", "our"
- Possessive language
- Self-referential
- Claiming credit
- Avoiding responsibility

### Communication Patterns
- Monologuing
- Not listening
- Interrupting
- Dismissing others' input
- Changing subject to self
- One-upping stories
- Minimizing others' achievements
- Exaggerating own accomplishments

## Relationship Red Flags

### Early Warning Signs
- Love bombing initially
- Moving too fast
- Excessive flattery
- Mirroring your interests
- Isolating from support system
- Testing boundaries early
- Small controlling behaviors
- Subtle put-downs
- Backhanded compliments

### Escalation Patterns
- Increasing criticism
- More frequent gaslighting
- Expanding control
- Reducing partner's autonomy
- Punishing independence
- Demanding more attention
- Less empathy over time
- More blame shifting
- Escalating manipulation

## Conflict Patterns

### During Arguments
- Must win at all costs
- Never apologizes genuinely
- Blames partner
- Uses past against partner
- Brings up unrelated issues
- Personal attacks
- Name calling
- Threats
- Stonewalling
- Walking away

### After Conflicts
- No genuine remorse
- Fake apologies ("I'm sorry you feel that way")
- Expects immediate forgiveness
- No behavior change
- Repeats same patterns
- Punishes partner for bringing up issues
- Denies conflict happened
- Rewrites what occurred

## Detection Indicators

### High Confidence Markers (80%+)
- Multiple gaslighting phrases
- Consistent dismissal of emotions
- Pattern of blame shifting
- Lack of genuine apologies
- Excessive "I/me/my" usage
- Control over major decisions
- Punishment for independence
- Requires constant admiration

### Moderate Confidence Markers (60-79%)
- Some controlling language
- Occasional dismissiveness
- Difficulty with empathy
- Needs validation
- Competitive in relationships
- Difficulty admitting fault
- Some manipulation tactics

### Emerging Markers (40-59%)
- Self-focused conversation
- Difficulty with criticism
- Needs to be right
- Some entitlement language
- Occasional invalidation
- Competitive tendencies
