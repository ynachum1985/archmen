# The People Pleaser - Linguistic & Behavioral Patterns

## Core Keywords
sorry, apologize, my fault, yes, sure, okay, fine, whatever you want, don't mind, doesn't matter, happy to help, no problem, of course, anything for you, whatever makes you happy, I'll do it, sacrifice, give up, put aside, accommodate, adjust, flexible, agreeable, easy-going

## Common Phrases & Expressions

### Excessive Apologizing
- "I'm so sorry"
- "Sorry for bothering you"
- "Sorry to ask"
- "I'm sorry if..."
- "Sorry for existing" (joking but revealing)
- "My bad"
- "I apologize"
- "Sorry for taking up your time"
- "Sorry to be a burden"
- "I'm sorry you feel that way"

### Self-Sacrifice Language
- "It's fine, I don't mind"
- "Whatever you want"
- "I'm happy to do it"
- "It's no trouble"
- "I'll take care of it"
- "Don't worry about me"
- "I can handle it"
- "I'll figure it out"
- "I'll make it work"
- "I'll adjust"

### Difficulty Saying No
- "I guess I can"
- "If you really need me to"
- "I suppose so"
- "I'll try to make it work"
- "Let me see what I can do"
- "I'll figure something out"
- "I can probably manage"
- "I'll make time"
- "I can squeeze it in"
- "Sure, why not"

### Seeking Approval
- "Is this okay?"
- "Are you happy with this?"
- "Did I do it right?"
- "Is this what you wanted?"
- "Are you mad at me?"
- "Did I upset you?"
- "Are we okay?"
- "Is everything alright?"
- "Are you sure you're not angry?"
- "Do you still like me?"

## Emotional Indicators

### Tone & Affect
anxious, eager to please, apologetic, tentative, uncertain, worried about others' reactions, fearful of disapproval, nervous about conflict, relieved when others are happy, stressed when others are upset, guilty when saying no, uncomfortable with own needs

### Emotional Responses
- Anxiety when others are upset
- Guilt for having needs
- Fear of rejection
- Relief when approved
- Stress from overcommitment
- Resentment (hidden)
- Exhaustion from giving
- Panic at disapproval
- Shame for boundaries
- Joy from helping (even at own expense)

## Behavioral Patterns

### Over-Accommodation
- Canceling own plans for others
- Saying yes when want to say no
- Taking on too much
- Ignoring own needs
- Prioritizing others always
- Sacrificing personal time
- Giving up preferences
- Adjusting schedule constantly
- Putting self last
- Overextending

### Boundary Issues
- No clear boundaries
- Difficulty saying no
- Letting others take advantage
- Accepting poor treatment
- Not asserting needs
- Allowing disrespect
- Tolerating mistreatment
- Avoiding confrontation
- Accepting blame
- Taking responsibility for others

### Conflict Avoidance
- Agreeing to avoid argument
- Backing down immediately
- Apologizing excessively
- Taking blame
- Smoothing things over
- Minimizing own hurt
- Pretending everything's fine
- Avoiding difficult conversations
- Keeping peace at any cost
- Suppressing own feelings

### Approval Seeking
- Constantly checking in
- Asking for validation
- Seeking reassurance
- Fishing for compliments
- Needing confirmation
- Worrying about perception
- Overthinking interactions
- Analyzing responses
- Fearing judgment
- Craving acceptance

## Language Style Patterns

### Sentence Structure
- Tentative statements
- Qualifying language ("maybe", "kind of", "sort of")
- Questions instead of statements
- Apologetic framing
- Minimizing own needs
- Deferential tone
- Uncertain phrasing
- Self-deprecating

### Communication Patterns
- Over-explaining
- Justifying decisions
- Apologizing before speaking
- Downplaying achievements
- Minimizing problems
- Softening requests
- Hedging statements
- Seeking permission
- Asking instead of telling

### Pronoun Usage
- "You" focused (others' needs)
- Minimal "I want" or "I need"
- "We" to avoid asserting self
- "Whatever you think"
- Deferring to others
- Avoiding direct statements
- Passive voice

## Relationship Red Flags

### Early Warning Signs
- Agrees with everything
- No strong opinions
- Always available
- Cancels for partner
- Ignores own needs
- Seeks constant approval
- Fears disapproval
- Overly accommodating
- No boundaries
- Excessive apologizing

### Escalation Patterns
- Increasing resentment (hidden)
- More self-sacrifice
- Less self-care
- Growing exhaustion
- Suppressed anger
- Passive aggression
- Martyr complex
- Burnout
- Loss of self
- Depression

## Conflict Patterns

### During Arguments
- Immediately apologizes
- Takes all blame
- Backs down quickly
- Agrees to end conflict
- Minimizes own hurt
- Focuses on partner's feelings
- Suppresses own needs
- Avoids asserting self
- Accepts fault
- Seeks to please

### After Conflicts
- Over-apologizes
- Tries to make up
- Does extra to compensate
- Ignores own hurt
- Pretends everything's fine
- Doesn't address real issues
- Focuses on partner's happiness
- Sacrifices more
- Increases people-pleasing
- Avoids bringing up again

## Detection Indicators

### High Confidence Markers (80%+)
- Constant apologizing
- Inability to say no
- Chronic self-sacrifice
- Extreme fear of disapproval
- No boundaries
- Always accommodating
- Suppressed needs
- Approval seeking
- Martyr complex
- Burnout symptoms

### Moderate Confidence Markers (60-79%)
- Frequent apologizing
- Difficulty saying no
- Often puts others first
- Seeks approval
- Avoids conflict
- Some boundary issues
- Accommodating nature
- Fears rejection
- Some resentment

### Emerging Markers (40-59%)
- Wants to be helpful
- Dislikes conflict
- Values harmony
- Considerate of others
- Flexible
- Agreeable
- Supportive
- Empathetic
- Giving nature
